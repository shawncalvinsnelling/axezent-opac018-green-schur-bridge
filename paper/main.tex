\[
P_\Phi = \operatorname{conv}(\Phi)

be the root polytope. For a root-spanned subspace $U$, let $\pi_U$ denote orthogonal projection and let $\Psi=\Phi \cap U$. The target containment is encoded by the gauge
\[

\[
