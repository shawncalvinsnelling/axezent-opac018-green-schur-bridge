# OPAC-018 Open Audit Items

These are the main questions an external referee may ask. They are intentionally visible rather than hidden.

1. **Global bridge validity**  
   Does the local rational/envelope control transfer to the full OPAC-018 projection setting under the stated hypotheses?

2. **Gauge identity scope**  
   Does every use of the `1/2 ||.||_1` gauge identity occur only inside a component where the hypotheses are satisfied?

3. **Non-simply-laced normalization**  
   Are short-root and long-root normalizations preserved without hidden floating-point or classification-table assumptions?

4. **Signed-graph structural coverage**  
   Does the signed-graph decomposition cover every root-spanned subspace claimed in the classical cases?

5. **Type B and Type D case split**  
   Are all configurations specific to Types B and D explicitly included or conservatively bounded?

6. **Cellini-Marietti transfer**  
   Does the convex-hull transfer preserve the strict margin `kappa < 2` in the intended cases?

7. **Oblique subspace stability**  
   Is the reduction stable under oblique choices of `U`, or is the current proof restricted to root-spanned/component-normalized subspaces?

8. **Asymptotic margin safety**  
   Are all limiting cases protected by an explicit strict-margin argument for finite rank?

9. **Exceptional-family boundary**  
   Are exceptional families excluded or treated only with clearly marked future-work status?
