# OPAC-016 Prior-Art / Novelty Boundary

This file intentionally separates proof validity from novelty/priority.

Relevant established background includes:

1. Thomas Zaslavsky, **Signed Graphs**, Discrete Applied Mathematics 4 (1982), 47–74. Signed-graph balance, switching, linear representation, and sign-symmetric examples related to classical root systems are established background.
2. Thomas Zaslavsky, **Signed Graphs and Geometry**, arXiv:1303.2770. Expository treatment emphasizing geometry and classical root systems.
3. Paola Cellini and Mario Marietti, **Root Polytopes and Borel Subalgebras**, IMRN 2015, 4392–4420, DOI 10.1093/imrn/rnu070. Root-polytope structure for finite crystallographic root systems.
4. Paola Cellini and Mario Marietti, **Root systems, affine subspaces, and projections**, Journal of Algebra 587 (2021), 310–335, DOI 10.1016/j.jalgebra.2021.07.035. Projection geometry is directly relevant background.

This release therefore does **not** claim that signed-graph switching, the Type-C cross-polytope description, or root-system projection methods are original.

Before claiming publication novelty, an expert should determine whether the exact standalone formula

`kappa(C_n,U)=1` when no balanced A-block occurs, and `kappa(C_n,U)=2-2/b_max` otherwise

already appears explicitly in prior literature or is an immediate corollary of an existing theorem.
